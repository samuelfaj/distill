# d1.2 as a decision model, and the step battery

Liquid AI released d1.2 and d1.2-ultra on 2026-11-16. Both are served on
OpenRouter's Decisions route, which takes the same typed envelope as Jev, and
on a separate Extractions route. This page records what was measured on
2026-11-17 before any default changed, which levers moved to which model, and
what is still unmeasured.

The measurements behind it, all under `scripts/benchmarks/`:

- `jev-decisions/billing_probe.py`: one probe of how d1.2 bills a battery.
- `jev-decisions/run.py` and `results-2026-11-17.json`: labelled sets for the
  effort, delegation and child-model questions, in the old and the new
  wording, on three models. `--rescore` scores a stored run again without
  requests.
- `jev-decisions/extraction_probe.py` and `extraction-2026-11-17.json`: field
  extraction on d1.2 and d1.2-ultra.
- `context-retrieval/selector_models.py` and `selector-models-2026-11-17.json`:
  the evidence selector's own contracts from the October report, replayed on
  three models.
- Two live tests in `crates/codegen/distill-workspace/tests/jev_live.rs`.

## Outcome

| Decision | Model by default | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Effort and tier per round (step battery) | `[jev] model` (Jev) | Same score on Jev and on d1.2-ultra. Awaited before every main round: Jev answered in about 0.6 s all day, d1.2-ultra in 0.65 to 3.2 s |
| Delegation hint (work shape) | `liquid/d1.2` | 12/12 on all three models; cheapest |
| Utility for an `explore` child | `liquid/d1.2` | 8/8 on all three models; cheapest |
| `ask_stored_output` evidence selection | `liquid/d1.2` | Found 20/20 evidence groups, against 16/20 for Jev, at 46% of Jev's cost |
| `jev_decide` fields | `liquid/d1.2` | Only d1.2 serves extraction; d1.2 and d1.2-ultra both read 21 of 27 fields right |
| Every other lever | `[jev] model` (Jev) | Not measured on d1.2 |

`[jev.models]` moves any of these (`b2_step_battery = "liquid/d1.2-ultra"`).

The question wording changed more than the model did. The new effort battery
asks what kind of step a call is and leaves the level to a table in code. The
old question asked the decision model to pick a level from the mode's prose,
and on this set it picked too low a level for hard steps.

## How d1.2 bills a battery

d1 and d1-ultra billed the whole state once per question. That is why the
October selector, with one question per candidate, cost 12.44 times Jev's
auxiliary charge on d1-ultra (`docs/d1-context-retrieval-results.md`).
OpenRouter's documentation says the same for d1 images ("for every question in
the request") and "once per request" for d1.2.

The probe sent the first 12,000 characters of `docs/jev-routing.md` with 1 and
with 8 `noul` questions:

| Model | 1 question | 8 questions | Latency |
|---|---|---|---|
| `liquid/d1.2` | 2,925 tokens, $0.0000585 | 3,203 tokens, $0.0000641 | 721 / 754 ms |
| `typesafe/jev-1.14` | 3,088 tokens, $0.0001297 | 3,377 tokens, $0.0001418 | 729 / 659 ms |

Both models bill the state once per request. On this probe d1.2 cost 45% of
Jev's charge. The client therefore accepts `liquid/d1.2`, `liquid/d1.2-ultra`
and their snapshots, and refuses `liquid/d1` and `liquid/d1-ultra`.

## Effort: the step battery against the effort `choice`

Two sets of hand-written states of a model call, each with the levels the
author judged right under Normal on a menu of low to max:

- **Steps (32).** A step, its last calls and results, and the request. The
  first 24 were used while the wording changed; the last 8 were written and
  labelled before they were run.
- **Short replies (24).** A request's first step where the request is "yes",
  "continue", "try again" and the like, some with the end of the assistant
  message they answer. The first 16 were used while the wording changed; the
  last 8 were labelled before they were run.

| | Jev 1.14 | d1.2 | d1.2-ultra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Old `choice`, 32 steps | 27 (5 too low) | 21 (11 too low) | 31 |
| Battery, 32 steps | **32** | 29 | **32** |
| Battery, the 8 steps labelled first | 8 | 7 | 8 |
| Old `choice`, 24 short replies | 18 (6 too low) | 18 | 22 |
| Battery, 24 short replies | **23** | 21 | **24** |
| Battery, the 8 replies labelled first | 7 | 6 | 8 |

The old question's score moves between runs: Jev scored 24, 26 and 27 of 32
on the same steps the same day. Every miss on the steps was too low. It
picked low or medium for the first step of a diagnosis, a design, a proof, a
destructive migration or a security review.

The battery's one miss on Jev is a held-out reply: "go ahead" to "I can run
the three failing tests one by one to see which change broke them" was read
as a lookup and ran at low, where the label asked for medium or high.

How the wording got here:

- **First step.** The model plans the whole request there, so the battery
  judges the request itself. Before that change every hard request was judged
  by its first tool call, a lookup, and ran at the cheapest level.
- **Edits and the costly bump.** An edit whose content is decided stays an
  edit across files. The bump follows the picked kind and stops at high.
- **Short replies.** The first battery called "yes" or "continue" an open
  design: on the first 16 replies it picked high for 12 on Jev, where the old
  question scored 13 of 16. The state now carries `replying_to`, the end of
  the assistant message the request answers, and an `unstated` kind runs a
  reply that shows no work at the mode's fallback level.

Per decision in the committed run:

| Model | Median latency (p90) | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Jev 1.14 | 616 ms (675) | $0.000040 |
| d1.2 | 649 ms (697) | $0.000012 |
| d1.2-ultra | 678 ms (1,038) | $0.000026 |

d1.2-ultra's median was 2.4 s in a run four hours earlier and 0.7 to 1.1 s in
three runs before that. Jev's stayed near 0.6 s in all of them.

## Delegation and children

**Delegation.** 12 requests, each labelled with the advice it should get:
none, delegate, delegate in parallel, investigate, or settle the decisions
first and then delegate.

- The old pair (`fits_single_call`, `needs_parallel`) scored 10/12 on Jev and
  d1.2-ultra and 11/12 on d1.2. It has one hint for sequential work, so an
  investigation label accepts its "delegate"; it cannot say "settle the
  decisions first".
- The work shape scored 12/12 on all three. One request is labelled
  investigate, and one accepts either delegate or investigate; the parallel
  investigation hint is not covered by any label.

Work-shape answers on d1.2 took a median of 0.69 s (p90 0.82 s; one of 12
took 1.6 s). The turn's first request waits at most 1.2 s from the ask.

**Children.** 8 assignments for a read-only child, each labelled `utility`
(find, list, quote, summarize) or `worker` (explain a cause, compare, judge a
risk). All three models scored 8/8.

## Evidence selection

The October report kept the exact state and include/exclude questions of 12
production selector calls: 3 synthetic tasks, 2 trials and 2 turns, with 20
evidence groups in all. Replayed unchanged on 2026-11-17:

| Model | Groups found | Candidates kept | Cost | Median (max) latency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Jev 1.14 | 16/20 | 12 | $0.0030638 | 776 ms (860) |
| d1.2 | **20/20** | 16 | $0.0014037 | 922 ms (1,113) |
| d1.2-ultra | **20/20** | 16 | $0.0029477 | 1,075 ms (1,401) |

Jev's result and its charge match the October report to the last digit, so
the replay reproduces the production call. The selector waits at most 3 s.

## Field extraction

27 fields over eight synthetic tool outputs (test logs, a build log, a git
log, a JSON status, a traceback, a Docker build, a log with accents). A field
counts as right when the value is the expected one and its span reads it back
from the text, or when it is `null` for a value the text does not hold.

| Model | Fields right | Cost | Median (max) latency |
|---|---|---|---|
| d1.2 | 21/27 | $0.0000518 | 688 ms (785) |
| d1.2-ultra | 21/27 | $0.0001087 | 762 ms (849) |

The misses, on both models:

- A stray character kept in the value: `src/parser.rs:212:9:`, `'u_5512`.
- A number glued to its unit or a symbol returned as `null`: `30s`, `31.2s`,
  `(#69)`.
- d1.2 once returned a present but wrong value: the image hash for the push
  digest the log does not hold. The span check cannot catch that.

So `jev_decide` reports where each value was read, and its description tells
the model to read that line before relying on the value.

## Live checks

`cargo test -p distill-workspace --test jev_live -- --ignored live_step_battery live_extraction`
ran green against OpenRouter with the final wording:

- **The battery on d1.2-ultra**, through the production client:
  - "read a file" was answered `lookup` and runs at low;
  - "find why the eviction test fails" was answered `diagnose` and runs at
    high.
- **Extraction on d1.2**:
  - the panic location came back with a span that reads it back;
  - the failure count came back as 1;
  - a deploy URL that is not in the log came back as an honest `null`.

## What is on, and how to turn it off

Every switch below is on by default, by the owner's rule for new efficiency
work. None has been through a paired task benchmark.

| Key | Effect | Off |
|---|---|---|
| `[jev.ladder] b2_step_battery` | Effort and tier per round come from the step kind and the tables in `catalog/battery.rs`. One answer serves the tier pass and the effort pass of a round. The request-wide B2 lowering pass is skipped where the battery asks. | `false` restores the effort `choice`, the tier `choice` and the lowering pass |
| `[jev.ladder] b6_work_shape` | With a worker, in a mode that allows subagents, the work shape is asked in the background when a request arrives. Its hint says what to hand over. | `false` restores the pair on the tool-family request |
| `[jev.ladder] b7_utility_children` | A fresh `explore` child whose assignment is plain reading runs on the utility model. Only when `[jev.local] model` is a catalog entry; with the shipped chain nothing is asked. | `false`, or `b7_subagent_model = false` |
| `[jev.models] <key>` | The decision model of one lever, `stored_output_search` or `jev_decide_fields`. | Name `~typesafe/jev-latest` |

The four decisions on `liquid/d1.2` send their state to Liquid through
OpenRouter instead of TypeSafe. An explicit `[jev] model` does not move them.

## Not measured yet

- **Task outcomes.** No paired benchmark of tasks, so there is no evidence yet
  that the battery saves main-model tokens or keeps the same pass rate. The
  old question's misses were all on the cheap side, so the battery spends
  more thinking on hard first steps than before.
- **The labels.** All labels are the author's; no second person checked them.
  40 of the 56 effort states were used while the wording changed.
- **Menus with `none` or `minimal`.** Every measured menu started at low.
- **Utility children.** Their reports are not compared with the worker's.
- **The work-shape hints.** It is not measured whether main models delegate
  more after them.
- **`jev_decide` fields.** Not measured beyond the 27 fields above.
